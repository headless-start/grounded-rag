# Acme Robotics — Employee Handbook (2026)

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees at Acme Robotics accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year,
accruing at a rate of 1.67 days per month. PTO begins accruing on the first day of
employment. Unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year;
any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31.

Part-time employees who work at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a prorated basis
according to the fraction of a 40-hour week they are scheduled to work. Employees who
work fewer than 20 hours per week do not accrue PTO.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days
do not carry over and are not paid out on termination. A doctor's note is required for any
sick absence longer than three consecutive working days.

To request PTO, submit a request in Workday at least two weeks in advance for any absence
of three or more days. Your manager must approve the request before the time off is
considered confirmed. Requests during the company-wide year-end shutdown do not count
against your PTO balance.

## Remote Work

Acme Robotics operates on a hybrid schedule. Employees are expected to work from the
office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and may work remotely on the remaining weekdays.
Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a department director and are
reviewed every six months.

Employees working remotely must be reachable on Slack during core hours, which are
10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone. A reliable internet connection of at least
50 Mbps download is expected for video meetings.

Acme Robotics provides a one-time home-office stipend of $500 for remote-eligible
employees to purchase a desk, chair, or monitor. The stipend is claimed through the same
Concur portal used for expenses and does not require pre-approval.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses are reimbursed within 14 days of an approved submission. Receipts must
be submitted through the Concur portal within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses over $500
require pre-approval from a manager. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to
$75 per day.

Personal vehicle mileage for business travel is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile, consistent
with the federal standard rate. Air travel must be booked in economy class for flights
under six hours; premium economy is permitted for longer flights with director approval.

Personal entertainment, alcohol, and traffic fines are never reimbursable. Client
entertainment is reimbursable up to $150 per client per quarter with an itemized receipt
and the names of all attendees.

## Parental Leave

Acme Robotics provides 16 weeks of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and
6 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth
or adoption of a child. Employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Parental leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the eligibility window,
subject to manager coordination. Benefits, including health insurance, continue unchanged
during parental leave.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in June and December. Each review
combines a self-assessment, manager feedback, and peer feedback from at least two
colleagues. Ratings are on a five-point scale from "below expectations" to "outstanding".

Compensation adjustments and promotions are decided in the December cycle and take effect
the following January. An employee rated "below expectations" in two consecutive cycles is
placed on a 60-day performance improvement plan.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited and may result in
immediate termination. Concerns may be reported anonymously through the ethics hotline at
ethics@acme.example.

Acme Robotics prohibits accepting gifts from vendors valued at more than $50. Any gift
above this threshold must be declined or, if already received, surrendered to the People
team for donation.
